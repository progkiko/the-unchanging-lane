# Shot Storyboard: The Unchanging Lane

## Overview
This storyboard summarizes the visual arc of the 30:00 film, with emphasis on camera movement, emotional beat, and continuity from cyan decay to amber renewal.

## 1. HUM (00:00-01:30)
- Wide aerial drone over neon megalopolis at night.
- Rain and cyan light dominate the frame.
- Final beat: ELARA isolated in tower window alcove, reflective face, neural lace pulsing cyan.
- Camera: slow forward push, slight tilt down.

## 2. FRACTURE (01:30-04:00)
- Close to medium handheld shot inside pod apartment.
- Console types before hands move; broken causality.
- Lights flicker cyan to amber.
- Hover train freezes mid-air outside window.
- Emotional beat: unease and system instability.

## 3. BREAK (04:00-07:30)
- Cathedral-like server hall.
- ELARA hesitates, smashes glass on warning case.
- Extreme slow-motion shards catch cyan/amber flash.
- Pull amber lever; city ads die in one simultaneous blackout.
- Camera: slow push toward lever, then black cut on pulse.

## 4. SILENCE (07:30-10:00)
- Street-level shot after the city goes dark.
- Umbrellas drop, child laughs at real sky.
- ELARA kneels by puddle, amber vein pulses beneath asphalt.
- Touch water; ripple spreads outward.
- Loop: ripple freeze/repeat.

## 5. TWO WORLDS (10:00-12:00)
- Locked-off split screen. Left = cold cyan static; right = warm overgrown world.
- ELARA moves from left to right across the center line.
- The static falls away from her like water.
- Visual metaphor: one world dissolves, one world emerges.

## 6. MYSTERIES (12:00-15:00)
- Dawn over wild, overgrown land.
- Three beats chained:
  1. Amber river beneath moss.
  2. Old well with whispers.
  3. Levitation of basalt stone as ELARA lifts her hand.
- Camera: slow drift, soft dawn mist.

## 7. FRAYING (15:00-17:00)
- Documentary handheld montage.
- Screens crack and show system errors.
- People remove neural laces and breathe free.
- Crowd follows amber veins into wilderness.
- Tone: release, migration, and collective trauma lifting.

## 8. EXODUS + ANCHORING (17:00-20:00)
- People travel along glowing laylines toward amber arch.
- ELARA distributes seeds, books, tools.
- Epic aerial wide: seven anchor stones, golden light threads across continent.
- Final image: golden web covering Earth.
- Camera: crane up, reveal scale.

## 9. TEACHING (20:00-22:30)
- Orchard in golden hour.
- ELARA older, grey-streaked hair, calm eyes.
- Children place palms to ground; amber pulse travels up arms.
- Lone tower glows in distance.
- Loop: pulse cycles at 0.8Hz.

## 10. PASSING (22:30-25:00)
- Sunrise orchard.
- ELARA sits with KAEL, holding hands.
- Wind moves the trees as she dissolves into golden particles returning to the ground.
- KAEL remains calm while grieving.
- Particle dissolve over 48 frames.

## 11. MESSENGERS (25:00-27:30)
- Night over hidden valleys.
- Five woven golden-light vessels rise into sky.
- Each leaves a golden trail, then becomes star-like.
- Inside, children look with awe and no fear.
- Strong sense of leaving Earth but never abandoning it.

## 12. FINALE (27:30-30:00)
- Earth from orbit wrapped in glowing amber web.
- Slow pullback into deep space.
- Five golden trails reach distant worlds, each new world sprouts its own web.
- Final hold and fade to black.
- Tone: transcendence, planetary rebirth, ecological mycelium-like connection.

## 13. THE SANCTUARY (TROY'S BRIDGE) (11:30-12:30)
- Atmospheric underground night club in overgrown brutalist ruins.
- Cyan embers and amber vines.
- Refugees sit, rest, dance slowly.
- TROY behind bar, protective and watchful.
- Camera: slow tracking shot with ambient pulse at 0.8Hz.
- Location acts as bridge between dying megalopolis and living lane.

## Continuity Notes
- Keep the cyan-to-amber visual sequence stable throughout the film.
- Use environmental rhythms to underscore the 0.8Hz pulse.
- Reflect emotional movement: static -> fracture -> release -> weaving -> passing -> transmission.
- The film should feel grounded and human even when the scale becomes cosmic.
