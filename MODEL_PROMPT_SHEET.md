# Model Prompt Sheet: The Unchanging Lane

## Universal Base Prompt
Cinematic sci-fi, anamorphic 2.39:1 look framed in 16:9, shallow depth of field, volumetric light, film grain, no text, no logos, no watermark. ELARA, woman, 28, dark shoulder-length hair, pale skin, wearing a dark utility jacket, a small glowing neural lace behind her left ear. [SCENE PROMPT]. [CAMERA MOTION]. [LIGHTING]. [NEGATIVE PROMPT: text, subtitles, watermark, logo, cartoon, distorted hands, extra fingers, morphing faces, flicker artifacts, jump cuts.]

## Clip Prompts Summary
### HUM
Sweeping aerial flight through a hyper-dense neon megalopolis at night, 2092. Glass towers dripping with cyan light, thousands of flickering holographic ads, cyan hover-trains carving light trails through smog, heavy rain hammering chrome surfaces. Slow forward drone move, endless rain, cyan palette dominant. Ends on a window alcove 400 floors up where ELARA stands alone, face reflecting the city, neural lace pulsing cyan. Slow push in toward her eye.

### FRACTURE
Interior of a small high-tech pod apartment. ELARA types on a glowing console, but the cursor and text appear on screen a moment before her fingers move. She pulls her hands back and the console keeps typing. Pod lights flicker from cyan to amber. Through the window, a hover-train judders and freezes mid-air. Tense, handheld-steady medium shot, slight push in.

### BREAK
A cathedral-like server hall. At its center, a single analog lever behind a glass case etched with warnings. ELARA raises a hammer, hesitates, then smashes the glass. Glass explodes in extreme slow motion, each shard catching cyan and amber light. She grips the glowing amber lever and pulls it hard. All holographic ads in the distant city snap off at once.

### SILENCE
Low-angle shot at street level, rain falling on a city street after the lights go dark. Umbrellas drop and clatter. A small child (6) looks up at the grey, real sky and laughs. ELARA kneels by a puddle, where a faint amber vein of light pulses under the asphalt. She touches the water and the amber light ripples outward.

### TWO WORLDS
A perfectly vertical split screen with a hard center line, same street on both sides. LEFT: cold cyan, rapid flickering light, panicked people clutching dead implants. RIGHT: the same street overgrown with moss and plants, quiet, warm amber glow pulsing slowly like a heartbeat. ELARA stands at the center, steps from left to right, and the cyan static falls off her like water. Locked-off wide shot.

### MYSTERIES
Dawn in wild overgrown land. A river of amber light flows beneath moss along an ancient path, ELARA places her palm on it. Then an old stone well with faint ghostly whispers drifting out. Then a levitating basalt stone, humming, lifting her hand weightlessly. Slow floating camera, soft golden dawn light, mist.

### FRAYING
Handheld montage in a dying city. Cracked cyan screens showing system errors. People pry glowing neural laces from behind their ears, then breathe deeply with tears of relief. A crowd walks out of the empty megalopolis, following faint amber veins into the wilderness. Desaturated cyan, documentary handheld feel.

### EXODUS + ANCHORING
A high mountain ridge at golden dawn. A trickle of people grows into a river flowing along glowing laylines toward a giant natural amber rock arch. ELARA stands at the arch handing out seeds, books and tools. Then epic wide aerial shots of seven glowing anchor stones on mountain peaks across a continent, golden threads of light shooting between them until a golden web covers the planet. Crane up, epic scale.

### TEACHING
A warm valley orchard at golden hour, trees planted in a layline pattern. ELARA, older, grey-streaked hair, calm eyes, sits among children. They press their palms to the ground, and a gentle amber pulse travels up their arms as they smile. In the far distance, a lone tower glows amber like a lighthouse.

### PASSING
Orchard at sunrise. Older ELARA, grey-streaked hair, sits with KAEL, a woman of 20, holding her hands. Wind moves through the trees. ELARA closes her eyes, her body shimmers and dissolves into golden particles that flow into the glowing ground. KAEL weeps but stays steady. The orchard glows brighter.

### MESSENGERS
Night over Earth's hidden valleys. Five vessels made of woven golden light, not rockets, rise silently, each leaving a golden trail. They pass a lone tower that flashes amber as a beacon, then accelerate upward and become stars. Cut inside to children aboard, awake, looking at the stars without fear.

### FINALE
Earth from orbit wrapped in a delicate golden web, glowing and breathing slowly. Slow orbital pullback. Five golden trails extend into new threads reaching distant worlds, and each world, once touched, sprouts its own amber web. A starfield of linked lights like mycelium. Beautiful, organic, not technological.

### THE SANCTUARY (TROY'S BRIDGE)
Atmospheric interior of The Sanctuary, an underground night club in overgrown brutalist ruins along the lane. Soft neon cyan embers reflect off wet stone while warm bioluminescent amber vines snake across the walls and moss-covered dance floor. Former city refugees in utilitarian clothing sit, rest, and dance slowly in deep peace, some gently touching the bare skin behind their ears where neural laces once glowed. At the center, TROY stands behind a heavy reclaimed timber bar, watching over the crowd like a guardian. In the background, rain gently washes over a cracked glass skylight. Slow steady tracking shot, 0.8Hz sub-bass ambient lighting pulse.

## Looping and Timing Notes
- Keep first and last frames aligned on motion loops.
- Use 0.8Hz pulse for environmental movement and light breathing.
- For split-screen and composite operations, create layers separately and assemble in post.
- Match project rhythm to 1.25s cycle whenever possible.
