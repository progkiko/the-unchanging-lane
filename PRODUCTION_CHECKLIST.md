# Production Checklist: The Unchanging Lane

## Core Delivery
- Runtime: 30:00 (1800 sec)
- Resolution: 3840x2160
- Frame rate: 24fps
- Aspect: Anamorphic 2.39:1 in 16:9 canvas
- Pulse: 0.8Hz = 1.25s cycle
- Output target: grayscale-ready for grade/pass, then final color pass

## Visual System
- Cyan = system decay, static, dead infrastructure
- Amber = living ecology, truth, memory, renewal
- Gold = anchoring, culmination, planetary web
- Sand/stone/earth = grounded humanity, roots, continuity

## Required Post Tasks
- Add overlay text where specified: DISCONNECT, SYSTEM DRIFT UNPRECEDENTED
- Composite split-screen shots separately from left/right halves
- Apply film grain and anamorphic flare in grade
- Use subtle motion blur or slight light flicker where needed
- Keep loop starts/ends matched for repeated shots

## Workflow
1. Generate base clips from prompt bible.
2. Check motion continuity for loop clips.
3. Composite split-screen and layered effects.
4. Add text overlays in Resolve.
5. Apply LUT pass / color grade and match frame-to-frame continuity.
6. Final review against emotional arc and runtime.

## Quality Controls
- No text, logos, or watermarks in final generated shots.
- Prevent distorted hands/fingers and face morphing through prompt discipline.
- Maintain character consistency for ELARA and TROY.
- Ensure energy transitions feel organic: cyan to amber to gold.

## Final Master Sequence
- 13 clip structure with final 30-second master
- One non-looping master at the end
- Black transition at end after 72f gold fade and 3s hold

## Deliverables
- AI generation source clips
- Resolve grade pass
- Final composite timeline
- Exported master sequence
- Storyboard and continuity notes
